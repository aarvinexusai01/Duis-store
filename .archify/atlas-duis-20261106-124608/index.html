Database (88 tables, every column)

  • 1/9 Identity and customersusers, sessions, accounts, profiles, addresses, staff invitations (10 tables).
  • 2/9 Catalog and mediadepartments, categories, attributes, products, variants, media (15 tables).
  • 3/9 Inventory and collectionsstock locations, levels, reservations, movements, collections (7 tables).
  • 4/9 Cart, wishlist and promotionscarts, wishlist, stock alerts, newsletter, discounts, coupons (8 tables).
  • 5/9 Ordersorders, items, discounts, status history, redemptions, COD remittances, notes (7 tables).
  • 6/9 Payments and walletpayments, refunds, webhook events, wallet ledger and credit lots (8 tables).
  • 7/9 Shipping, exchanges and reviewsshipments, tracking, exchange requests, customer photos, reviews (10 tables).
  • 8/9 CMS, page builder and journalrevisions, pages, navigation, redirects, blog (11 tables).
  • 9/9 Settings, operations and privacystore settings, tax and FX, audit logs, notifications, consent (12 tables).

Flows and state machines

  • Checkout and paymentQuote, place order, pay, and the browser, webhook and reconciler paths into one idempotent settle.
  • Order statuspending_payment to delivered, expiry, late payment, cancellation and returns to origin.
  • Shipment statusForward-only courier states ranked 0 to 8, with terminal states.
  • Exchange requestApproval, price difference, reverse pickup, QC and replacement.
  • CMS revision workflowdraft, review, schedule, publish and archive.
  • Operation and background-job executionauthorize, validate, one transaction, pg-boss job, retries, dead letter, cache revalidation.
