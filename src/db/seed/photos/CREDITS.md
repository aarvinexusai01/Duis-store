Photos from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com), used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Each link opens the photo page, which names the photographer.

**Development and demo use only.** The people pictured are Pexels models, not Duis customers or ambassadors, and the Pexels License forbids implying they endorse a product. The garments are generic stand-ins chosen by type and nearest colour, not Duis products. Replace them with your own product photography before launch (doc/10 A16).

- `npm run db:seed` uses these photos for a new development database.
- `npm run db:seed:photos` swaps them into an existing one, keeping its accounts and orders.
- `node scripts/fetch-demo-photos.mjs` downloads anything listed in `manifest.json` that is missing.
